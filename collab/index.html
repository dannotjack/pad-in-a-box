The musical brief · G, space and expression

Moonlight adds warmth and expression for verses and choruses. Dawn builds towards bigger choruses and bridges. Strings are welcome, but so are other instruments and sounds.

Compose in G: G and D are your main anchors; A and E add openness. Use B thoughtfully for a more major sound. C and F♯ add colour and can create tension. Leave space for other parts and live musicians—a pentatonic starting point works well.

Export with the beginning and ending silence you want heard. Recordings keep their original pitch and speed. Try combinations; there is no fixed song length or shared beat to follow.
