Troubleshooting

No sound?

Press Start Audio. Check device volume and your speaker or headphone output. Still silent? Reload and try again.

Why the delay?

Root and atmosphere changes wait one second after your last tap. The sound then blends gradually.

No strings or organ?

Strings play in Moonlight and Dawn; organ plays in Dawn. Leave time for phrases and swells. Check the display for an audio error.

Where are the knobs?

Knobs hide when the screen is too short. Rotate your device or use a larger screen to reach them.

Audio stops when I lock my phone.

Keep the page open and your screen awake. Switching apps can interrupt playback.

Can I use a minor key?

Choose the song’s root. Start with Midnight; melodic parts in other atmospheres may clash with some chords.

Are settings saved?

Settings stay in this browser when storage is available. They don’t sync across devices. Clearing browser data removes them.

About Pad in a Box

pad in a box.

not jack.

A little space for music to breathe.

I created Pad in a Box because I wanted something simple: a musical companion that could play alongside me.

As a musician and worship leader, I wanted something that could sit quietly behind my guitar, build with the music, and keep playing when I put my instrument down.

Inspired by Brian Eno's approach to generative ambient music, Pad in a Box brings together sounds that evolve, interact and occasionally surprise us.

It's not designed to take the lead, but to support the moment.

Whether you're leading worship, playing on your own, or simply taking time to reflect, I hope it gives you a little more space to listen, create and breathe.
