Getting Started

Music that plays with you.

Pad in a Box creates evolving musical atmospheres designed to support your playing, worship, or moments of reflection.

1. Choose your key

Select the musical key using the keyboard.

2. Choose your atmosphere

Use the left and right arrows to move between Dusk, Midnight, Moonlight and Dawn.

3. Make it yours

Adjust the knobs to shape the music around what you're playing.

The music evolves naturally, so you can focus on playing.

There's no need to play every note. Let the music breathe.

The Four Atmospheres

Each stage has its own musical purpose. Choose the atmosphere that best supports the moment.

Dusk — Rest and reflect

PAD + GENERATIVE PIANO

Let the music play for you. Gentle, evolving piano notes create a peaceful atmosphere.

Ideal for quiet meditation, reflection, and moments when you want to stop playing and simply listen.

Midnight — A foundation to play on

PAD

A calm, sustained musical foundation that leaves space for your own instrument.

Ideal for accompanying gentle verses, prayer, and quieter musical moments.

Moonlight — Add expression

PAD + STRINGS

Expressive cello and violin phrases add movement, warmth, and musical embellishments.

Ideal for bigger verses and choruses that benefit from a little extra expression.

Dawn — Build dynamically

PAD + STRINGS + ORGAN

A rich, full musical backing with expressive strings and swelling organ.

Ideal for big choruses, bridges, and moments that need a powerful dynamic foundation.

Move freely between atmospheres. The music transitions smoothly as you change stages.

Your Controls

Everything you need to shape the music is right at your fingertips.

Playing

Start / Stop Audio

Tap Start Audio between the arrows to begin. Tap Stop Audio there whenever you need silence.

Your chosen key and atmosphere stay selected when the music stops.

Keyboard

Tap a note to change the key of the music. Pad in a Box adjusts the instruments to follow your selection.

The keyboard selects your musical root note. It isn't intended for playing individual notes or melodies.

Atmosphere Buttons and Arrows

Tap Dusk, Midnight, Moonlight or Dawn directly, or use the arrows to move between them.

The app briefly waits for your final selection before changing. Musical transitions happen gradually.

Shaping the Sound

Fade — Default: 8 s

How smoothly the music changes.

Controls how gradually previous sounds fade away when you change atmospheres or keys.

Longer fades create gentler transitions. Shorter fades respond more quickly.

Movement — Default: 35%

How much the pad moves.

Adds subtle movement and variation to the sustained pad sound.

Keep it low for a steadier atmosphere, or increase it for a more evolving texture.

Tone — Default: 68%

How bright or warm the music feels.

Shapes the overall sound using a filter.

Turn it down for a darker, softer sound, or up for a brighter, more open sound.

Volume — Default: 55%

How loud Pad in a Box plays.

Controls the overall output volume of the instrument.

Adjust this to sit comfortably alongside your guitar, piano or other instruments.

Drag a knob upwards to increase its value or downwards to decrease it. On desktop, double-click a knob to restore its default.

Reading the Display

The display shows your current atmosphere, selected musical key, and playback status. The waveform moves with the sound.

Interactive Help — Explore Controls

Open Help

Open Help, then choose Explore Controls.

Tap a highlighted control

A small explanation appears. Inspecting a control shouldn't accidentally change the music.

Exit Help mode

All controls return to their normal behaviour. Music continues playing throughout.

Playing Together

A companion for your music.

Whether you're playing alone or with a worship team, Pad in a Box is designed to support your music and create space for the moment.

1. Playing on your own

Start with Midnight and select your song's key. The pad provides a steady musical foundation while you play guitar, piano or another instrument.

2. Building dynamics

Move into Moonlight for bigger verses and choruses that need more expression. Switch to Dawn for powerful choruses and bridges. Return to Midnight when you want to settle the music.

3. Taking a break

Put down your instrument and switch to Dusk. Let the generative piano continue playing while you rest, pray or create space for quiet meditation.

4. Moving between songs

Keep the music flowing between songs. Use Midnight for a gentle background, and change the key when you're ready to move into the next song. The sound transitions gradually.

5. Playing with a worship team

Let Pad in a Box fill the gaps rather than compete with the band. Adjust the volume and tone so it sits comfortably alongside other instruments, especially keyboards and strings.

A little tip

Pad in a Box follows your selected root note, not your chord changes. Think of it as a musical foundation, rather than another musician following every chord.

Questions & Troubleshooting

Need a hand?

Here are a few quick answers to help keep the music flowing.

I can't hear anything.

Tap Start Audio between the arrows, check the Volume knob and your device's volume, and make sure the sound is going to the correct speakers or headphones. If it still doesn't work, reload the page and try again.

Why doesn't the atmosphere change immediately?

Pad in a Box briefly waits for your final selection before smoothly blending between atmospheres. This prevents sudden changes in the music. You can adjust the Fade knob to control how gradual transitions feel.

Why can't I see the knobs?

On smaller screens, the knobs may be hidden to keep essential playing controls accessible. Your existing settings are preserved. Use a larger screen or change your device's orientation to access them when space allows.

Why does the music stop when I lock my phone?

Some mobile browsers pause audio when the screen locks or you switch apps. For more reliable playback, keep the browser open and your screen awake.

Why isn't the music following my chords?

Pad in a Box follows your selected root note, not individual chord changes. It creates a musical foundation you can play over, rather than automatically following every chord.

Why can't I hear the strings or organ straight away?

The instruments evolve naturally over time. Moonlight introduces expressive string phrases, while Dawn adds swelling organ sounds. Some parts need a little time to develop.

Are my settings saved?

Yes. Your settings are saved locally in your browser. They won't automatically sync between devices, and clearing browser data may remove them.

About Pad in a Box

pad in a box.

by not jack.

A little space for music to breathe.

I created Pad in a Box because I wanted something simple: a musical companion that could play alongside me.

As a musician and worship leader, I wanted something that could sit quietly behind my guitar, build with the music, and keep playing when I put my instrument down.

Inspired by Brian Eno's approach to generative ambient music, Pad in a Box brings together sounds that evolve, interact and occasionally surprise us.

It's not designed to take the lead, but to support the moment.

Whether you're leading worship, playing on your own, or simply taking time to reflect, I hope it gives you a little more space to listen, create and breathe.
