Organ drawbars

Pull down to draw out: 0 is quiet, 8 is full. Brown subharmonics, white octaves, black upper harmonics. The organ holds 1 and 5; 2 fades in and out.

Master filters & motion

Tone is a master low-pass filter (180 Hz–20 kHz), including reverb. Cello cutoff uses a steeper 24 dB/octave filter. Movement smoothly increases drone drift, filter motion and stereo spread; zero settles the pad. Fade sets outgoing stage tails and the overall key-change duration. Dawn enters quickly for its fast Leslie cycle: fast 2–4 s, slow 4–10 s.
